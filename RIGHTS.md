# Asset rights and attribution

This repository is public. Add only images that you are authorized to distribute publicly on GitHub, or for which you have explicit permission covering that use. Keep a short record of the source and rights basis in `manifest.json` when available.

Approval to use an image in an internal presentation does not automatically grant permission to publish it publicly. Do not upload confidential, customer-specific, or otherwise restricted material. If public distribution rights are unclear, leave the image out until they are confirmed.
